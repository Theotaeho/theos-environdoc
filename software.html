EcoLPT

Lagrangian Particle Tracking tool for Ecohydrology · v1.0 · developed with the Water Systems Modeling Group, Oak Ridge National Laboratory

Conventional Eulerian models resolve states and fluxes at fixed locations. EcoLPT complements them by reconstructing water history: it follows water from precipitation through coupled surface and subsurface pathways to evapotranspiration or watershed export, quantifying its origin, trajectory, residence time and path length.

This Lagrangian view exposes water connectivity, transport pathways, storage selection and hydrologic memory — properties that cannot be inferred from states and fluxes alone, and that matter for understanding how water moves through and sustains an ecosystem.

Model-agnostic

Designed as a post-processing tool, so it can be applied to output from an existing Eulerian model rather than requiring a new one.

Mesh-flexible

Supports both structured and unstructured meshes, matching the grids that integrated watershed models actually use.

Parallel & extensible

Parallel computation for large domains, with customization and functional extension built into the design.

Manuscript in preparation for Geoscientific Model Development. Validated against a physics-based watershed simulation built from Amazon rainforest field observations.
