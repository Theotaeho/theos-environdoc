Hi! I’m Taeho Kim, an Ecohydrologist working to observe, understand, and diagnose the Earth system.

Through my Field-to-Earth philosophy, I seek to understand how the Earth system works and how it is changing. I connect 1) field observations to capture how ecosystems behave in the real world, 2) process understanding to identify the mechanisms that shape these dynamics, and 3) Earth system modeling to scale these insights and explore how ecosystems may respond to a changing climate.

Let’s make big waves together!

01

Research Interests

Ecohydrology

Understanding how water moves through the soil–plant–atmosphere continuum and how these processes shape ecosystem and Earth system dynamics.

Hydroclimatic Extremes

Identifying hydroclimatic extremes, their drivers, and their impacts on ecosystem water and carbon dynamics across scales.

Ecosystem Resilience

Understanding the drivers of ecosystem vulnerability and resilience across environmental gradients and under future change.
